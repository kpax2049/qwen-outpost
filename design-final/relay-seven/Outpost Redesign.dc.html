OutpostVisual redesign — phase 1Direction for approval

FOUNDRY.

A cohesive art direction for Outpost: inked, machined forms on warm dusk terrain, where every machine is identified by silhouette and every state by shape, not just colour. Same simulation, same costs, same rules — a new visual language wrapped around them.

01 — What's there today

One Canvas2D surface (Renderer.ts) draws a 120×120 world at 48px tiles. Terrain is five procedurally-tinted flat tiles from generateTerrainTiles (L73): speckled grass, forest with one trunk and two canopy discs, #c4a35a sand, #1a4a7a water with an animated shoreline, #6a6a6a rock. All eight buildings come out of one drawBuilding (L597) fed only by BUILDING_DEFS[type].color + .shape — four rects, two diamonds, one circle, one arrow. Status lands as a corner dot, a thin bar, a count bubble and a red frame. Seven DOM panels sit on top from App.css: #1a1a2e panels on a #0f1020 backdrop.

02 — The real weaknesses
  • Shape collides four ways. .shape has only four values for eight buildings: Storage, Chest, Smelter and Steel Furnace are all rect; Miner and Assembler are both diamond. Type rests on hue.
  • …and hue collapses into three clusters. Greens: storage #5b7a4f, chest #7a9a5f, assembler #00aa44. Orange-reds: generator #cc4400, smelter #ff4400. Blue-violets: miner #6666aa, steel #4444cc. So the two signals collide on the same pairs.
  • Greens fight the ground. Storage, chest and assembler are green objects sitting on green grass; the world's most common terrain is the same hue as three buildings.
  • Rich engine states, thin visuals. The engine already distinguishes Mining / Smelting / Crafting / Transporting, Waiting for Input, Output Blocked, No Power, No Fuel and No Resource Below — but all of it lands as one 6px corner dot in three colours plus an optional red frame.
  • Chrome is a third palette. #1a1a2e panels on #0f1020 are a violet-navy that belongs to neither the warm world nor the status colours, and ok/warn/bad double as UI colours.
03 — The direction

Chunky machined objects with a hard 2px ink outline and two-tone flat shading — the discipline of 16-bit sprite work, the weight of stamped industrial hardware, sitting on a warm, hand-layered dusk landscape. Code-drawn primitives only: clip-path silhouettes, flat fills, one bevel, one shadow.

▸ Silhouette carries type. Colour confirms it.
▸ State is a collar + a badge shape.
▸ Motion only where the sim is moving.
▸ World is warm. Chrome is cool. Never mixed.

GEOMETRY & MATERIAL
  • Tile grid 48px. All machine geometry snaps to a 4px sub-grid.
  • Ink outline: 2px at 48px tiles, scaling with zoom, colour #101418.
  • Two-tone flat shading, hard terminator at 46% on a 160° axis. No gradients in the world beyond this.
  • One drop shadow per object: 8% down, 4.5% blur, rgba(8,12,16,.45).
  • Corner radius: 3px machines, 4px containers, full round for generators. Panels 4px.
MOTION LANGUAGE
  • Movement means throughput. Belt treads scroll only while items advance; a stopped belt is visibly still.
  • Glow means output. Working machines carry a soft status halo; nothing else glows.
  • Pulse means attention. 1.2s ease-in-out breathe, reserved for blocked and no-fuel.
  • Power sparks travel generator → consumer at a fixed 1.6s cadence, independent of sim speed.
  • All UI transitions 120ms; no easing tricks, no bounce.

MACHINE STATE MATRIX — at true 48px gameplay size
Every state is three redundant signals: a collar (solid / dashed / dotted), a badge shape (chevron, double-bar, struck bolt, empty gauge), and a fill treatment (glow, hatch, desaturation). Colour is the fourth signal, never the only one — so the board survives colour-blindness and greyscale.

CONVEYORS, TURNS & ITEMS
A belt is a treaded band with a static direction wedge at its centre. WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE
Every cost, power figure, capacity and recipe on this board is read from BUILDING_DEFS (src/types/index.ts L263–387) and every state from computeBuildingStatus (GameEngine.ts L983) — unchanged. Item fills are ITEM_COLORS verbatim. The restyle touches only BUILDING_DEFS.color/.shape, Renderer.ts draw code and the panel CSS — never GameEngine.ts.
